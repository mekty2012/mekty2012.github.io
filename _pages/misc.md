Coffee
======
Specialty coffee is one of my hobbies. I brew by hand both at home and in the lab. When I visit other places, I often make cold brew with specialty beans, which has earned reactions like "we can't taste this anywhere else." 
If you are planning to meet me, I can prepare this for you, but please let me know in advance.
Lately, I have also been experimenting with cold brew made with spirits such as vodka and whiskey.

## Cafes I recommend

### Daejeon

- **[Little Town Coffee Stand](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&hl=en&query=리틀타운커피스탠드+대전+유성구+어은로42번길+25)** (near KAIST) --- A warm, inviting place with lovely hand drip and americano. It is often full, but the bar seats are just as good, thanks to the welcoming baristas. Its first store is Midtown Coffee Stand.
- **[Contra Coffee](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&hl=en&query=콘트라커피+대전+유성구+어은로52번길+1)** (near KAIST) --- Clean and modern, with good espresso.
- **[Gabae Roasters](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&hl=en&query=가배로스터스+대전+유성구+어은로42번길+21-10)** (near KAIST) --- Warm and woody. It doesn't specialise in hand drip, but its hand drip is still solid. The salty drinks are also worth trying.
- **[CHILLIF Espresso Bar](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&hl=en&query=칠리프+대전+유성구+어은로57번길+35)** (near KAIST) --- Small, woody, and charming, with lovely espresso and espresso-based drinks.
- **[Champspace Coffee Roasters](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&hl=en&query=챔프스페이스+커피로스터스+대전+동구+수향길+97)** (near Daejeon Station) --- Fairly large and beautifully designed, with lovely hand drip, great espresso-based drinks, and coffee cocktails. The owner, Min-Seo Kang, placed 5th at the 2018 World Coffee in Good Spirits Championship, a coffee cocktail competition. On a hot summer day, try the shakerato.
- **[Black Desk](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&hl=en&query=블랙데스크+대전+중구+선화동+374-5)** (near Jung-gu Office Station) --- Spacious and modern, serving several funky coffees.

## Coffee Beans I prefer

### Ethiopia Heirloom Washed

A beautifully floral cup with mild acidity and a clean finish, and excellent value for its quality. Ethiopia's high altitude is usually what makes this profile possible.

### Costa Rica Typica Honey

Rich sweetness and fruity flavours in a clean cup, though it is usually a little expensive. My favourite is San Isidro Labrador Typica Honey.

### Peru Geisha Washed

A tea-like, floral cup with mild acidity, and quite affordable compared to other Geisha coffees.

### Colombia Sudan Rume Natural

I don't usually prefer highly fermented coffees like this one, but Sudan Rume excels with its silky texture and sweet flavour.

## Gear

### Grinders

- Comandante C40 MK4 --- Lately I have come to think it isn't worth its price, despite its fame. I still recommend it, because many roasteries use it as their standard reference for grind size.
- 1Zpresso K-Ultra
- Timemore Chestnut S5
- NineAM M1 Pro
- Fellow Ode Brew Grinder Gen 2 --- Highly recommended. It is quite expensive but worth the price, giving a clear and vivid cup.

### Brewers

- Hario V60 Ceramic Immersion Dripper Switch (Goryeo celadon limited edition)
- Hario V60 Dripper NEO
- ORIGAMI Dripper (ceramic)
- CAFEC Flower Dripper Deep 27
- Tarachine Conical 30
- Hario V60 Dripper SUIREN
- AeroPress
- BeanPlus cold brew maker --- Highly recommended. Other cold brew makers need ice to stay cold, but this one is compact enough to fit in a small refrigerator. It is also easy to clean.

### Others

- **Scale:** Acuba Glowgram --- Highly recommended. It shows the flow rate per second, which revealed how irregular my pouring was and makes it a great tool for practising a smooth pour.
- **Kettle:** Fellow Stagg EKG Pro (600 mL)
- **Cups:** Refined 2-WayCup (130 mL and 330 mL)
- **Tumbler:** Fellow Carter Carry Tumbler --- Highly recommended. Unlike most tumblers, it has a ceramic-coated interior that keeps metallic flavours out of the coffee.
- **TDS meter** Atago PAL-Coffee (BX/TDS)
