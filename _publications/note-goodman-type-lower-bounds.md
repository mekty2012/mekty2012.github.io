summary: >-
  Proves the bound $$t(H,W) \ge \Phi_H(p)$$, where $$\Phi_H$$ interpolates the densities of $$H$$
  in balanced complete multipartite graphons, for chordal graphs whose maximal cliques have equal
  size, trivially perfect graphs, and joins of a clique and an odd cycle.
  Shows that a nonbipartite graph satisfies it at all sufficiently high edge densities if and only
  if its girth is odd, and classifies the 117 nonbipartite graphs on at most six vertices with no
  isolated vertices or isolated edges, with the density ranges for the 23 that violate the bound.
