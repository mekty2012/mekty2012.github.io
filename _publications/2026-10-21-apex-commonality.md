summary: >-
  We prove that $$H^{+k}$$, the graph $$H$$ with $$k$$ independent vertices joined to every
  vertex of $$H$$, is common for every $$k \ge 1$$ when $$H$$ is a tree or an even cycle,
  establishing two infinite families of a conjecture of
  [Grzesik, Lee, Lidický, and Volec](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0963548322000074),
  and formalise the proofs in Lean 4.
