summary: >-
  We prove $$t(C_m, W) \ge p^m - p(1-p)^{m-1}$$ for every odd $$m \ge 3$$
  and every graphon $$W$$ with edge density $$p = t(K_2, W) \ge 2/3$$,
  and formalise this bound in Lean 4.
