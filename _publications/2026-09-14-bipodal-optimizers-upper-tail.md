title: "Bipodal optimizers in the upper-tail variational problem for regular subgraph densities"
authors: "Sangho Lim, Seonghyuk Im, **Taeyoung Kim**, Kyeongsik Nam, Hongseok Yang"
venue: "arXiv preprint arXiv:2609.15222"
note: "Submitted to Random Structures & Algorithms"
date: 2026-09-14
arxivurl: "https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15222"
summary: >-
  For the upper tail of $$d$$-regular subgraph densities in $$G(n,p)$$, we prove that near the replica-symmetric phase boundary the optimizer of the Chatterjee--Varadhan variational problem is bipodal and unique, and derive asymptotic expansions of the rate function.
