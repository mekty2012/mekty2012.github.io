---
title: "An Infinite-Width Analysis on the Jacobian-Regularised Training of a Neural Network"
authors: "**Taeyoung Kim**, Hongseok Yang"
venue: "International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML 2024)"
date: 2024-07-27
paperurl: "https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/kim24ah.html"
arxivurl: "https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03386"
summary: >-
  We show that an MLP and its Jacobian jointly converge to a Gaussian process in the infinite-width limit, and that training with a Jacobian regulariser is then governed by a linear ODE determined by a variant of the Neural Tangent Kernel.
