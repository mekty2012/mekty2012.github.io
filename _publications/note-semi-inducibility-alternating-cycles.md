summary: >-
  Proves $$t(C_{2m}^{\mathrm{alt}}, W) \le 4^{-m}$$ for every graphon $$W$$ and odd $$m \ge 3$$,
  and the refinement $$t(C_{2m}^{\mathrm{alt}}, W) \le (p(1-p))^m$$ when the edge density $$p$$
  lies in $$[(5-\sqrt5)/10, (5+\sqrt5)/10]$$.
  The first bound also follows from
  [Grzesik et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22189) on directed cycles.
