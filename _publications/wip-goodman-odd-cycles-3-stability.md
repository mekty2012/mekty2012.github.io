summary: >-
  If $$\lvert t(K_2, W) - p_k \rvert \le \delta_e$$ and $$t(C_m, W) \le t(C_m, W_k) + \delta_C$$,
  where $$p_k = 1 - 1/k$$ for an integer $$k \ge 3$$ and $$W_k$$ is the balanced complete
  $$k$$-partite graphon, then $$\delta_\square(W, W_k) = O(\sqrt{\delta_e} + \sqrt{\delta_C})$$;
  moreover, the exponent $$1/2$$ is optimal.
