% ================================================================ interests
\section{Research Interests}
Machine learning for mathematics, especially combinatorics: deep-learning frameworks for
variational optimisation problems arising in extremal graph theory and in large deviations of
random graphs, and more broadly for characterising extremisers of functional inequalities in
harmonic analysis and PDEs. Earlier work on the theory of deep learning: infinite-width limits,
in-context learning, and feature learning.
